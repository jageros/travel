## Why this place is worth visiting

The cross-lake journey is part of what gives Isle Royale its character. Once you are on the island, a walk can take you between sheltered woods and an open stretch of Lake Superior shore. You can appreciate that contrast on a short visit if you plan around the landing your boat or seaplane actually serves. The [National Park Service overview](https://www.nps.gov/isro/index.htm) links to its planning newspaper, The Greenstone, and to the park's safety information. Use the [official visit planner](https://www.nps.gov/isro/planyourvisit/index.htm) to get oriented before comparing ways across the lake.

## What to see

Choose **Rock Harbor** on the northeast end if you want to spend time looking out over Lake Superior. The [park's places guide](https://www.nps.gov/isro/planyourvisit/placestogo.htm) lists Scoville Point, a rocky outing from the Rock Harbor area. Check the [NPS day-hiking guide](https://www.nps.gov/isro/planyourvisit/day-hiking.htm) for route details and pick a turnaround point before setting off. A shorter shore outing gives you time to stop and watch the water while leaving a margin for the return to the dock.

Choose **Windigo** on the southwest end if you are drawn to Washington Harbor and the woods around it. The [official Windigo guide](https://www.nps.gov/isro/planyourvisit/windigo.htm) links to a map and describes trails of different lengths. Pick a walk from the landing that fits the time you actually have. If the weather or your group's pace calls for a shorter visit, spend time by the harbor and choose a route closer to the arrival point.

## Who it suits

Isle Royale suits visitors who enjoy remote landscapes and can leave some flexibility in their plans. For a first trip, one arrival area helps you focus on the shoreline and forest instead of trying to fit both ends of the island into the same visit. If people in your group have different walking abilities, discuss trail surfaces and how long you can be away from the landing. If a specific ferry, facility or route is essential, check it with the relevant operator or the park before booking.

## What to check before going

Start with the [NPS transportation guide](https://www.nps.gov/isro/planyourvisit/directions.htm) to confirm your mainland departure point, island landing and return. Check park access for your dates, how to reserve transport and which time zone your operator uses. Review [park conditions](https://www.nps.gov/isro/planyourvisit/conditions.htm) and plan supplies and clothing for a possible weather delay, even on a day trip. If staying overnight, confirm permits, lodging or camping and current food-storage rules. Leave a margin between the end of your walk and your departure, and make time to reach the return boarding point.
