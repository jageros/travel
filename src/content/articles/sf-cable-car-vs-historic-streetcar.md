San Francisco has two very different historic-looking rides that visitors often group together. A cable car is pulled by a cable beneath the street. An F Market & Wharves vehicle is a historic streetcar running on the F-line route. Decide which experience you want before opening a map, because the lines, boarding locations and route pages are different.

The distinction also keeps your plan honest. A photograph of an F-line streetcar does not show a cable car, and seeing a vintage vehicle does not identify the service by itself. Use the operator's current pages for the route and boarding check immediately before you travel.

## Start with the vehicle, not the landmark

Use this quick identification checklist:

| If you want to… | Look for… | Open this official page |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ride a cable car | A vehicle pulled by a cable beneath the street, with a cable-car line name | [SFMTA Cable Cars](https://www.sfmta.com/getting-around/muni/cable-cars) |
| Ride the waterfront historic streetcar | The F Market & Wharves route, its stop list and direction | [SFMTA F Market & Wharves](https://www.sfmta.com/routes/f-market-wharves) |
| Reach a specific sightseeing area | The line's current route and the stop nearest your destination | The matching SFMTA route page |

SFMTA identifies three cable-car lines. California runs between California and Market and Van Ness Avenue. The Powell lines begin at Powell and Market and continue toward the Fisherman's Wharf area. Those are cable-car route descriptions, not a description of every historic vehicle operating in the city.

The F-line page describes a route between the Castro and Fisherman's Wharf through Market Street, the Ferry Building and the Embarcadero. Its listed stops include the Ferry Building, Pier 39 and Fisherman's Wharf. The route page separates the two directions, so read the direction heading instead of searching only for a familiar place name.

## Check the boarding point and direction

For a cable car, SFMTA directs riders to a turntable at the beginning or end of a line or to a brown-and-white cable-car signpost. The page also says that cable cars are not equipped with accessible boarding. Treat that statement as a planning constraint and verify a different service when the intended journey requires accessible boarding.

For the F-line, open the route page and select the direction that matches your trip. The page provides separate inbound and outbound stop lists, stop IDs and links for real-time arrival predictions. A stop on the Embarcadero in one direction is not automatically the boarding point for the return. Save the exact stop, street and direction you plan to use.

Before leaving your hotel, write down four items:

1. The vehicle type you intend to ride.
2. The official route page for that service.
3. The exact boarding stop and direction.
4. The destination stop and the route you will inspect for the return.

If your destination is Pier 39 or Fisherman's Wharf, compare the F-line stop list with the entrance you actually need. If your destination is a cable-car line, check the line page and the signposted boarding point rather than using an F-line stop as a substitute. A nearby landmark can be served by more than one mode while still requiring a different walk and boarding decision.

## Keep the ride choice separate from the fare choice

This article identifies the service. Use the [Muni pass comparison](/guides/is-muni-pass-worth-it/) for ticket and pass arithmetic. A cable car and an F-line historic streetcar may both appear in a sightseeing plan, but their route and boarding checks remain separate. Do not add a pass calculation here or assume that a payment product answers the mode question.

The F-line page can show a historical vehicle in a photograph or list a current stop without promising that a particular car will arrive on your trip. Likewise, the cable-car page describes the system and its lines without guaranteeing a specific vehicle, queue length or boarding place at a particular moment. Reopen both official pages, inspect any current notices and confirm the return before you set out.
