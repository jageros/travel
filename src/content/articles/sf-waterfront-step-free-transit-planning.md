A check of the F Market & Wharves route can tell you where the streetcar is listed to stop. A step-free trip also depends on the way to the platform, how you board, where you get off and how you reach the entrance you need. Fill out this worksheet before choosing a departure. Treat anything you cannot verify as an open question.

## Match the stops to each direction

Open the [F-line stop lists](https://www.sfmta.com/routes/f-market-wharves) and read the direction headings. The route serves the Ferry Building and Pier 39 areas, but the two directions can use different stops. Enter the stop name and ID for each ride. At Pier 39, look up the opposite-direction stop before committing to the route back from your chosen entrance.

| Check | Outbound ride | Return ride |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Boarding and exit | Direction, stop names and IDs at both ends | Look up both stops for the opposite direction |
| Street approach | From your starting point to the boarding platform | From your destination entrance to the return platform |
| After you get off | From the exit platform to the entrance you will use | From the exit platform to your final destination |
| Open questions | Equipment, crossing or entrance to confirm | Write down any different requirement for the way back |

## Check the platform and the streetcar

Use [SFMTA's historic streetcar access guide](https://www.sfmta.com/getting-around/accessibility/muni-accessibility/muni-access-guide/access-historic-streetcars) to find the boarding arrangement for each named stop. Keep a ramp to a wayside platform separate from a lift at a designated stop. At a wayside platform, the guide says to use the ramp and wait for the operator to place a bridge plate. Tell the operator your exit stop and whether you need help with securement. Save any questions about the vehicle or equipment for SFMTA. A description of the boarding procedure gives you something specific to confirm for the ride you plan to take.

## Verify the route to the entrance

Check the street-level approach at both ends. Mark the crossings and curb ramps you would need, and ask the venue which entrance to use if the main entrance has steps. Check the path from that entrance to the return stop as its own route. A wheelchair user might need to confirm space inside the car, securement and the approach to the platform. If you are traveling with a stroller, identify where you would need to keep it rolled and where you could fold or carry it. Check the details that matter to your group.

## Recheck any change before you leave

Read the [temporary stop information](https://www.sfmta.com/travel-updates/temporary-stop-relocations) and [current transit updates](https://www.sfmta.com/travel-transit-updates). Open notices that match your route and direction and check their effective dates. If a notice moves your stop, repeat the platform and street-approach checks at the temporary location. If a necessary detail is still open, confirm it with SFMTA or the venue before relying on that leg. Keep an alternative that meets the same access needs for both directions.
