## Why this place is worth visiting

The original Space Shuttle Atlantis gives a visit to Kennedy Space Center a strong focal point. It's displayed at an angle with its payload doors open, so you can take in the shape of the spacecraft and imagine an astronaut seeing it in orbit. Look for the extended robotic arm, then spend time with the surrounding displays on what it took to work aboard a shuttle. The [Atlantis exhibit guide](https://www.kennedyspacecenter.com/explore-attractions/space-shuttle-atlantis) helps you choose what to look for.

## What to see

Decide whether your group would rather stay with the displays on the main campus or make time for the visitor complex bus tour. In the first case, give **Atlantis** time of its own. Then choose a second exhibition from the [official attractions directory](https://www.kennedyspacecenter.com/explore-attractions). The visitor complex organizes its attractions into zones that follow different eras of spaceflight, which is a useful way to find a subject you will want to spend time on.

If your interest is the Moon landings, put the **Apollo/Saturn V Center** high on the list. The official directory describes it as part of the Behind the Gates bus tour. Build that tour into your day deliberately, allowing for the boarding and return as well as time in the gallery. That choice may be more rewarding than trying to cover every zone.

## Who it suits

This outing is particularly good for travelers who enjoy engineering, science museums or the history of space exploration. For a mixed-age group, agree on an anchor exhibition and choose one optional activity everyone can decide on later. Visitors who prefer quieter exhibits or need a particular kind of seating or access should use the official facility and accessibility information to shape the day.

## What to check before going

Use the [official visit planner](https://www.kennedyspacecenter.com/info/plan-your-visit) to confirm the visitor entrance, parking, hours and security rules. If you are counting on a bus tour or a specific experience, check its current schedule, admission conditions and any booking requirements. Map to the public visitor complex for your arrival.

Keep launch viewing as a separate choice. Read the [operator's events information](https://www.kennedyspacecenter.com/launches-and-events) for anything tied to your planned visit. The spacecraft exhibits are a reason to go in their own right, and they give the day a focus if an event changes.
