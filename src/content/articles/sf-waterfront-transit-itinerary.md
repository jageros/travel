For a flexible waterfront outing, start with the Ferry Building and choose either Pier 39 or Fisherman's Wharf as your main destination. Make the other stop optional. Use the F-line historic streetcar for a leg you want to ride, then decide how much walking suits your group before committing to the return.

This is a route-planning framework, with places to shorten the day. It does not assume a particular arrival time, attraction booking or walking pace. Start with your accommodation-to-waterfront connection if you are still organizing the wider trip; our [car-free San Francisco guide](/guides/san-francisco-without-a-car/) covers that decision.

## Choose the short outing or the longer version

Pick the place you would be most disappointed to miss. Give that visit the largest share of your available time, and leave a margin for the journey back. A reserved activity, meal or onward connection should determine when you turn around.

| Your priority | Suggested outline | What to leave optional |
| --- | --- | --- |
| One waterfront destination and a streetcar ride | Ferry Building area, then a ride toward Pier 39; plan the return from there | Continuing into Fisherman's Wharf |
| A broader waterfront outing | Ferry Building area, Pier 39 and a selected Wharf destination | Extra stops between the places you chose |
| Conserving walking energy | Choose one destination and plan transit in both directions | A waterfront walk that would use the energy needed for the trip home |

These are choices about scope, rather than promises that every listed place will fit into a half day. Check any attraction's opening conditions separately. Decide what to drop before setting out, so shortening the outing still feels like completing the plan.

## First leg: Ferry Building toward Pier 39

Open [SFMTA's F Market & Wharves route page](https://www.sfmta.com/routes/f-market-wharves) and select the direction toward Fisherman's Wharf. Find the Ferry Building boarding stop, then identify your intended alighting stop. Check the direction displayed on the vehicle before boarding; a familiar route letter alone is not enough.

For the Pier 39 portion, use the route page's Pier 39 stop entry as a starting point for locating the destination. Compare the stop with the actual entrance you want to reach. A stop named for an attraction still leaves a street-level approach to plan.

Choose one of two patterns: ride this leg and explore near the destination, or walk a portion you have checked and save the ride for later. If riding a historic streetcar is a priority, allow for waiting without scheduling an immediate timed activity after arrival. The photograph shows a historical F-line vehicle; it does not promise that particular car will operate on your trip.

## Second leg: decide whether to continue into the Wharf

At Pier 39, reassess your remaining time and walking energy. Continue only if you still have room for the chosen Wharf visit and the return. Stopping here is a complete short outing.

If you continue, enter the specific destination address in your planner. Compare the walking route with another F-line leg, including the approach to each stop. Do not add a boarding simply to complete a list of stops. Equally, do not start walking because the next place looks close on a small map.

| Situation at the decision point | Useful next step |
| --- | --- |
| You want to look along the waterfront and everyone is comfortable continuing | Review the walking path and agree on the next stopping point |
| Someone needs to preserve energy for the destination or return | Check transit before adding more walking |
| Waiting would jeopardize your onward commitment | Shorten the visit and compare a feasible return journey |
| Walking is not a suitable alternative for someone in the group | Confirm a usable transport option before leaving your current location |

Walking is an optional part of this itinerary. It cannot serve as everyone's fallback. If the planned ride becomes unsuitable, dropping the extension may be more useful than trying to reach another attraction by an unplanned route.

## Save a different stop for the return

The F-line route page separates the direction toward Fisherman's Wharf from the direction toward the Castro. Inspect both lists. The Wharf portion uses different streets by direction, so returning to the place where you got off can put you at the wrong boarding point.

The names below are **examples to locate on the current official route page**, rather than a substitute for checking your departure point and travel date:

| Area | Entries toward the Wharf | Entries to inspect for the return |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ferry Building | The Embarcadero/Ferry Bldg | The Ferry Building entry in the opposite direction is a separate stop |
| Pier 39 | The Embarcadero & Stockton St (Pier 39) | Beach St & Stockton St; check the path from your chosen exit |
| Wharf extension | Jefferson St & Powell St or Jefferson St & Taylor St | Beach St & Mason St or Jones St & Beach St; select using your actual destination |

Save the return stop's location and direction, then plan the remaining journey to your accommodation or next appointment. Returning to the Ferry Building is one option; use the complete journey planner result to decide where you should actually leave the F line. Do not keep riding to a familiar landmark if it makes the rest of your journey harder.

## Check access at every boarding and exit

Use [SFMTA's historic streetcar access guide](https://www.sfmta.com/getting-around/accessibility/muni-accessibility/muni-access-guide/access-historic-streetcars) alongside the route page. It describes vehicle differences and boarding arrangements; a route being listed does not confirm that a particular journey meets your needs.

Check four points: the first boarding stop, the destination exit, the return boarding stop and the final exit. For each, establish the approach from the sidewalk, the boarding arrangement and the route onward to the entrance you need. Ask the operator to confirm an unresolved boarding question before relying on the journey.

Keep these distinctions in your plan:

- **Ferry Building and Pier 39:** locate the exact stop and direction first, then consult the access guidance. An attraction label does not verify the whole approach to its entrance.
- **Wharf extension:** inspect the Jefferson Street arrival and Beach Street return separately. Do not extend a statement about Embarcadero stops to every stop in the Wharf area.
- **A downtown connection:** check the specific connecting stop too. Do not assume that another stop on the same route has the same arrangement.

This guide has not verified the current operation of boarding equipment, curb access, pedestrian crossings or attraction entrances in person. If a necessary access detail remains unconfirmed, choose a shorter outing or seek a confirmed alternative before departure. A general walking suggestion does not resolve an accessibility gap.

## Make the last check before you leave

Reopen the official route page for service information and the selected stop entries. A missing arrival prediction does not establish a cancellation. Read the effective date of any advisory rather than treating every notice displayed on a stop page as current.

Save your main destination, optional extension, return stop and onward journey together. Agree on a point to turn back if energy or weather changes. Check the return again before starting the optional Wharf extension.

Once those legs are settled, use the [Muni pass comparison](/guides/is-muni-pass-worth-it/) if you need to evaluate a ticket product. Count the travel you actually plan to take. Adding extra rides to justify a pass can make a simple waterfront outing unnecessarily complicated.
