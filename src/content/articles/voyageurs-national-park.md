## Why this place is worth visiting

On a first visit to Voyageurs National Park in northern Minnesota, start with a choice: do you want to see the lakes from shore or spend part of the day on the water? From shore you can take in the forests and rocky outcrops that frame the water. On a boat, you can see how the shorelines, bays and islands fit together. The [National Park Service overview](https://www.nps.gov/voya/index.htm) is a starting point for seeing what kinds of trips are possible and where to look for current conditions.

## What to see

For a visit by land, plan around the **Rainy Lake Visitor Center** or the **Ash River** area. Near Rainy Lake, the [Rainy Lake Recreation Trail](https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/rainy-lake-recreation-trail.htm) is paved and passes forest, lake and marsh scenery. In the Ash River area, the [Kabetogama Lake Overlook Trail](https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/hike-kabetogama-lake-overlook-trail.htm) is an option if you want a viewpoint to anchor your outing. A shoreline stop and one walk give you time to see the details before moving on.

For a visit on the water, the [park's activities guide](https://www.nps.gov/voya/planyourvisit/things2do.htm) describes ranger-led boat tours and lists independent boating options. Read the descriptions to see where an outing begins and what it covers. If you choose a guided trip, leave time before and after it for a shore stop near that departure point. If you are choosing a hike instead, check how you reach its trailhead. Some trails that look close on the map require a boat.

## Who it suits

Voyageurs suits travelers who enjoy forest-and-water scenery and prefer a day with room for a stop or two. For a group with different interests or walking preferences, pick a visitor center and one land-based outing everyone wants to share. Treat a boat trip as a separate choice. For anyone who needs a particular trail surface or boarding arrangement, review the information for the exact site or tour and ask the park if you need details.

## What to check before going

The [official visit planner](https://www.nps.gov/voya/planyourvisit/index.htm) links to hours, maps, reservations, safety and accessibility. Confirm which site or launch your directions lead to. Check current conditions and weather for your chosen activity, especially if it depends on wind or water conditions. For a boat trip, use the park's navigation and boating safety guidance before committing to a route. Check the actual departure point and reservation process for a guided tour. Keep a land-based plan in reserve if the water portion no longer fits the day.
