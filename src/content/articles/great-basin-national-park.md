## Why this place is worth visiting

Great Basin packs very different landscapes into one park. In Lehman Caves, you can look closely at formations such as cave shields and stalactites. Higher on the mountain, the bristlecone pines and openings in the forest give you a reason to slow down and look around. After dark, the sky offers another way to experience the park. The [National Park Service overview](https://www.nps.gov/grba/index.htm) introduces the cave and the mountain trails. Start by choosing the experience you want most, then leave room for a second only if conditions cooperate.

## What to see

Choose **Lehman Caves** if you want to spend part of the visit underground. Use the [official cave tour guide](https://www.nps.gov/grba/planyourvisit/lehman-caves-tours.htm) to compare routes and learn what each involves. Check the stairs, footing and any low or narrow passages before committing. Give your group enough time to reach the check-in point and leave the rest of your schedule flexible around the tour you can get.

Choose the **Wheeler Peak high country** if you want to spend more time outside. The [Wheeler Peak trail guide](https://www.nps.gov/grba/planyourvisit/wheeler-peak-scenic-drive-trails.htm) describes outings to alpine lakes and a bristlecone pine grove. If the road and your chosen route are suitable, pick one of those destinations and allow time to look at the trees or lakeshore. For a different focus, use the [park's activities guide](https://www.nps.gov/grba/planyourvisit/things2do.htm) to plan some time under the night sky. Settle on a location and think through the return route while it is still light.

## Who it suits

The park suits travelers who like geology, mountain scenery or stargazing. A group with different interests can choose one shared activity and decide later whether to add another. Some visitors may be comfortable walking outdoors but uneasy about enclosed spaces, while others may prefer a tour to an uphill hike. Ask what everyone is comfortable doing before choosing, and look up the park's accessibility information if a specific route, surface or facility matters to anyone in the group.

## What to check before going

Use the [official visit planner](https://www.nps.gov/grba/planyourvisit/index.htm) and [current conditions page](https://www.nps.gov/grba/planyourvisit/conditions.htm) to confirm road access, weather, trail conditions and vehicle restrictions before setting out. Compare cave tour availability, reservations and check-in details for your dates. For an evening outside, review the forecast and choose a place where nighttime access is permitted. If your preferred road, tour or trail is unavailable, plan a different stop whose access you have confirmed.
